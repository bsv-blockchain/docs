The BSV blockchain implements the original Bitcoin protocol. BSV is a fork of the Bitcoin blockchain, sharing the ledger history of Bitcoin back to 2009, and runs on the <Hint tip="Unspent transaction output">UTXO</Hint> model.

On BSV, your app can verify transactions without running a node and leave the private keys with the user's wallet.

## What you can build

Apps on BSV put value, ownership or proof in transactions:

## Packages for building on BSV

BSV has one package per layer:

- `@bsv/sdk` covers hashing, keys, Script, <Hint tip="Background Evaluation Extended Format (BRC-62)">BEEF</Hint> and Merkle paths.
- `@bsv/simple` adds helpers for payments and tokens.
- `@bsv/wallet-toolbox` is for building a wallet.

To compare them and pick one, see the [SDKs overview](/sdk/).
