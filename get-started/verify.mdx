At this stage, you already sent a BSV testnet payment, from [Pay from a web app](/get-started/quick-start-browser/) or [Pay from a server](/get-started/quick-start-server/), and have its transaction ID. Now check that payment outside your wallet, on the testnet explorer. In this tutorial, you:

- Open the payment on the testnet explorer.
- Follow it from unconfirmed to confirmed.
- Read the fee it paid.
- Check the payment from code.

Looking up a payment does not change your wallet or your balance.

## Before you begin

{/* WRITER: when Pay from a server is written, check where it prints the txid and name it here. */}

Have the transaction ID of your testnet payment. In Pay from a web app, the `pay.mjs` script prints it. In BSV Desktop, click the payment under **Activity**, then **Copy ID**.

## How it works

The testnet explorer at [test.whatsonchain.com](https://test.whatsonchain.com/) reads the public testnet and shows any transaction by its ID. A new payment shows as unconfirmed until a miner includes it in a block, and from then on it shows the block and the number of confirmations.

## Open the payment on the explorer

Add your transaction ID to the end of the explorer address and open it in a browser. Replace `YOUR_TXID` with the transaction ID of your payment:

```text
https://test.whatsonchain.com/tx/YOUR_TXID
```

In BSV Desktop, click the payment under **Activity**, then **View transaction**, to open the same page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/verify/01-desktop-payment-details.png" alt="The Payment details window of BSV Desktop with the amount, the Accepted status, the transaction ID, and the Copy ID and View transaction links" />
</Frame>

<Callout type="note">
  For the first minute or two after a payment, the page can say **This page cannot be found. 404**, because the explorer has not listed the payment yet. Wait a minute and reload. If it still says so, check that the link starts with `https://test.whatsonchain.com/tx/`: the explorer at `whatsonchain.com`, without `test.`, is the Mainnet explorer and does not show testnet transactions.
</Callout>

## Follow the payment until it is confirmed

The transaction page changes once the payment is in a block. Take these steps to follow it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the unconfirmed payment">
    Right after the payment, the page shows **Status** as **UNCONFIRMED**, with **Fee offered to miner**, **Fee Rate**, **Size**, and the inputs and outputs. In the outputs, you find the amount you sent and the address it went to.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/verify/02-explorer-unconfirmed.png" alt="A WhatsOnChain testnet transaction page with the UNCONFIRMED status, the fee offered to miner and the size" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload until the payment is confirmed">
    Reload the page from time to time. Once the payment is in a block, the **Status** line gives way to **Timestamp (utc)** and **Block** with its number, and the page shows **Confirmations** and **Fee Paid** in place of **Fee offered to miner**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/verify/03-explorer-confirmed.png" alt="The same transaction page after confirmation, with the timestamp, the block number, the fee paid, the size and 13 confirmations" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

On testnet, the first confirmation can take from under a minute to about 20 minutes, depending on when the next block is mined.

<Callout type="note">
  BSV Desktop can still show **Accepted** while the explorer already shows confirmations, and changes to **Confirmed** later. The explorer shows the state of the testnet.
</Callout>

## Read the fee

The fee on the explorer is the same fee you approved in your wallet:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the fee on the explorer">
    The fee is next to **Fee offered to miner**, or **Fee Paid** once confirmed, in BSV. Multiply it by 100,000,000 to get satoshis, so `0.00000116` BSV is 116 satoshis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare it with BSV Desktop">
    In BSV Desktop, the **Spending Request** you approved showed the total with the fee, for example 1,116 satoshis for a payment of 1,000. The difference is the same fee as on the explorer. For a payment to your own address, the balance first goes down by the whole total, and BSV Desktop adds the amount back after you click **Get paid** on the **Wallet** page, open the **BSV ADDRESS** tab and click **Check for payments**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check the payment from code

The explorer also answers requests from code, so your app can check a payment without opening the page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the transaction">
    In a terminal, request the transaction from the WhatsOnChain testnet API. Replace `YOUR_TXID` with your transaction ID:

    ```bash
    curl https://api.whatsonchain.com/v1/bsv/test/tx/hash/YOUR_TXID
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the response">
    The response is JSON. Once the payment is in a block, it includes fields such as these:

    ```json
    {
      "txid": "<txid>",
      "size": 464,
      "blockheight": 1763389,
      "confirmations": 13
    }
    ```

    While the payment is unconfirmed, the response has no `blockheight`. Amounts in the outputs are in BSV, and the response has no fee field, so the explorer page is the quickest place to read the fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same request works from your own app. The `pay.mjs` script of [Pay from a web app](/get-started/quick-start-browser/) shows the simplest form, a link to the explorer built from the transaction ID.
