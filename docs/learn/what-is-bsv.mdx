The **BSV** blockchain implements the original Bitcoin protocol. Forked from Bitcoin Cash in 2018, BSV runs on the UTXO model.

On BSV, your app can verify transactions without running a node and leave the private keys with the user's wallet.

## What You Can Build

Apps on BSV put value, ownership or proof in transactions:

- Payments go through the user's wallet, which creates the transaction.
- Tokens are UTXOs tracked by an <Hint tip="Built from topic managers, which decide what is admitted, and lookup services, which query what was admitted.">overlay service</Hint>, which applies rules you define, such as for a redeemable voucher.
- Identity comes from the user's wallet: its identity key and certificates let your app know who it deals with, without accounts or passwords.
- Data integrity works by putting a hash of your data in a transaction and checking it later against its Merkle path and block header.

To add one of these to an app, see the [Guides](/docs/guides/).

## Packages for Building on BSV

BSV has one package per layer. `@bsv/sdk` covers hashing, keys, Script, BEEF and Merkle paths. `@bsv/simple` adds helpers for payments and tokens. `@bsv/wallet-toolbox` is for building a wallet. To compare them and pick one, see the [SDKs overview](/docs/sdk/).
