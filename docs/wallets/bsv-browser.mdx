BSV Browser is the BSV Association's wallet for the phone. It is a web browser for iOS and Android with a self-custodial wallet inside, so a BSV web app that you open in it can ask the wallet for payments and identity through <Hint tip="BSV Request for Comments 100, the standard interface between apps and wallets">BRC-100</Hint>. Read this page to decide when to use it instead of [BSV Desktop](/docs/wallets/bsv-desktop/), and to find where its main settings are.

## When to use BSV Browser

Use BSV Browser when you want a wallet on your phone, or when you want to open a BSV web app on a phone. Use BSV Desktop when you build and test apps on your computer, as the guides in these docs do.

| | BSV Desktop | BSV Browser |
|---|---|---|
| Runs on | macOS, Windows and Linux | iOS and Android |
| How apps reach the wallet | Apps on the same computer, through ports on your machine | Web apps opened inside the browser |

Download BSV Browser from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/bsv-browser/id6755637932) or from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.bsvassociation.browser). The [BSV Browser site](https://mobile.bsvb.tech) has the same links.

## Browser mode and wallet mode

BSV Browser starts as a normal web browser, with tabs, bookmarks and history, and no wallet. Nothing is created until you choose to. When you create or import a wallet, the browser switches to wallet mode, and BSV web apps can request payments, identity and certificates from it. A permission sheet shows each request, and you approve or reject it.

## Where things are

<Callout type="warning">
  Creating a wallet makes a recovery phrase that anyone can use to spend your coins. Keep it offline and never share it. Check **BSV Network** in **Wallet**, **Settings** before you receive or send coins: Mainnet moves real BSV, and testnet is for learning.
</Callout>

Open the menu with the three dots at the bottom right of the screen, then choose **Wallet**.

| To do this | Go to |
|---|---|
| Create a wallet | **Pay** or **Get paid**. The wallet is created the first time you use one of them |
| Import a wallet | **Import from backup**, at the bottom of the wallet screen |
| Switch network | **Wallet**, **Settings**, **BSV Network** |
| Back up the key | **Wallet**, **Settings**, **Backup Wallet Keys** |
| Export the wallet data | **Wallet**, **Settings**, **Export Wallet Data** |

## Networks

**BSV Network** offers Mainnet, Testnet and Teratest. Switching the network rebuilds the wallet on the chain you choose. Use testnet to learn and test, since its coins are free and have no value. A payment on Mainnet moves real BSV and cannot be undone.

## Back up your wallet

<Callout type="warning">
  Your keys are only on your device, and nobody else holds them. If you lose the phone and have no backup, you lose the wallet. Anyone with your recovery phrase can spend your coins, so keep it offline and never share it.
</Callout>

BSV Browser gives you four ways to back up. Use more than one.

- The recovery phrase: 12 words that restore your keys on any compatible device. Find it in **Backup Wallet Keys**. It restores only the keys, not the history
- Recovery shares: **Print Recovery Shares** splits the key into three printed pages, and any two of them restore the wallet. To restore, choose **Import from backup**, then **Scan Backup Shares**, and scan two pages. Like the phrase, they restore only the keys
- Wallet data: **Export Wallet Data** saves the wallet database as a `.db` file, with the transaction history, the coins and the certificates. To restore it, choose **Wallet**, **Settings**, **Import Wallet Data** and pick the file
- Private backup: by default, BSV Browser keeps an encrypted copy of the wallet database on a BSV Association server. It brings back the history on a new phone after you restore the keys with the phrase or the shares. The copy is encrypted on your device with a key that your phrase restores, so BSV Association cannot read it and cannot restore it for you. Turn it off or delete it in **Wallet**, **Settings**, **Private backup**. With it off, the phrase alone may not recover all of your funds on a new device
